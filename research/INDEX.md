## 2. In-Depth Analysis of Scouted 2025–2026 Research

### Paper 14: Reconstructing Impaired Language Using Generative AI for People with Aphasia
* **Authors:** Achini Adikari, Damminda Alahakoon, Miranda L. Rose, et al. (*Scientific Reports*, 19 Nov 2025).
* **Why This Research Was Chosen (The X-Factor):**
  Prior work in aphasia technology relied on rigid grammar drills. Adikari et al. demonstrate that LLMs configured via **in-context few-shot prompting** can dynamically reconstruct conversational utterances from telegraphic word fragments, word-finding gaps, and paraphasias without requiring expensive retraining or fine-tuning.
* **What + How It Is Directly Implemented in Lisan:**
  * **File:** [`src/lib/aiProvider.ts`](file:///Users/alhamdulillah/codespace/lisan/src/lib/aiProvider.ts) (`reconstructAphasicIntent`), [`src/components/SentenceBuilder.tsx`](file:///Users/alhamdulillah/codespace/lisan/src/components/SentenceBuilder.tsx), and [`src/components/modals/IntentConfirmationModal.tsx`](file:///Users/alhamdulillah/codespace/lisan/src/components/modals/IntentConfirmationModal.tsx).
  * **Mechanism:** When an aphasic user inputs 1–3 fragmented tokens (e.g. `[چائے, میٹھا]`), Lisan prompts the model to generate exactly 3 candidate complete sentences (direct request, inquiry, state). Crucially, **the UI enforces human-in-the-loop confirmation**: the patient taps their intended message before TTS vocalization.
* **Special Notes for Medical Organizations & SLPs:**
  * Preserves patient autonomy and communicative intent. Eliminates the catastrophic failure mode where an unconstrained LLM hallucinates an unintended statement.

### Paper 15: Technology‐Based Interventions in Aphasia Rehabilitation: Systematic Review
* **Authors:** Bader Alhejji, Stuart Cunningham, Samantha Dorney, Rebecca Palmer (*IJLCD*, 8 Sep 2026).
* **Why This Research Was Chosen (The X-Factor):**
  This landmark review analyzed 67 quantitative studies (including 14 RCTs) and discovered the **"Ecological Generalization Gap"**: computerized exercises and drills reliably produce improvements on trained test items, but these gains consistently fail to generalize to real-world everyday conversations.
* **What + How It Is Directly Implemented in Lisan:**
  * **File:** [`src/components/DailyLifeDock.tsx`](file:///Users/alhamdulillah/codespace/lisan/src/components/DailyLifeDock.tsx) and [`src/types/index.ts`](file:///Users/alhamdulillah/codespace/lisan/src/types/index.ts).
  * **Mechanism:** Lisan transitions away from sterile decontextualized flashcards to an immediate **Daily-Life Situational Dock** with 5 high-frequency ecological scenarios: Mealtime & Hydration, Comfort & Positioning, Medical & Pain, Family & Emotional Connection, and Urgent Needs.
* **Special Notes for Medical Organizations & SLPs:**
  * Aligns directly with the ASHA-FACS (Functional Assessment of Communication Skills) mandate for everyday functional independence.

### Paper 16 & 18: Speech Biomarkers for Dementia & Foundation Model Limits
* **Key Literature:** *Journal of Voice* Systematic Review (63 studies, 18,540 participants, 2026) & Chlasta et al. (*Frontiers in Neuroinformatics*, 5 Dec 2025).
* **The Reality Check:**
  The *Journal of Voice* review proves that while distinguishing Alzheimer's from healthy controls in isolated datasets looks promising, reliably distinguishing Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) from normal cognitive aging fails external cross-validation. Furthermore, Chlasta et al. showed that an ICASSP 2025 multi-modal pipeline combining HuBERT speech embeddings and GPT-4o achieved an F1 score of only 0.5521 on an independent test set.
* **Strategic Stance for Lisan:**
  * **We reject deceptive "AI Dementia Diagnostic" claims.** Consumer apps claiming to diagnose Alzheimer's from microphone recordings violate FDA SaMD guidelines and produce severe false-positive anxiety.
  * **Our Defensible Opportunity:** A non-diagnostic **Longitudinal Communication Journal** (Ticket 009) that objectively logs communicative interactions, Time-to-Expression (TTE) latency, and functional category trends for outpatient clinical review without diagnostic speculation.

### Paper 17 & 25: Dementia Caregiver Psychoeducational Support
* **Key Literature:** Peled-Cohen & Reichart (*TACL*, 2025) & Cheng & Ng (*JMIR Aging*, 6 Jan 2025).
* **The Reality Check:**
  Peled-Cohen synthesized 240+ papers and proved that academic literature is overwhelmingly skewed toward automated dementia detection, leaving family caregivers profoundly underserved. Cheng & Ng proved with 21 family caregivers that grounding AI advice in structured psychoeducational caregiving manuals (*Positive Dementia Caregiving in 30 Days*) provides measurable stress relief and communication scaffolding.
* **What + How It Is Directly Implemented in Lisan:**
  * **File:** [`src/components/modals/CaregiverGuideModal.tsx`](file:///Users/alhamdulillah/codespace/lisan/src/components/modals/CaregiverGuideModal.tsx) and [`src/lib/data/caregiverGuidance.json`](file:///Users/alhamdulillah/codespace/lisan/src/lib/data/caregiverGuidance.json).
  * **Mechanism:** Delivers 4 offline clinical caregiving modules (Aphasia Scaffolding, Sundowning De-escalation, Pain Identification, Caregiver Respite) and an optional conversational advisor strictly bounded by validated geriatric caregiving guidelines.

---

### Paper 19: Future Technologies in AAC & The Non-Negotiable Human Agency Rule
* **Authors:** Alessandra D. Benevento, Giuseppe Ciulla, Gianluca Merlo (*Frontiers in Communication*, 28 Oct 2025).
* **The Clinical Principle:**
  *"AI should suggest messages, not silently decide what a person meant."*
* **What + How It Is Directly Implemented in Lisan:**
  * Lisan's predictive engines, Markov transitions, and LLM intent reconstruction modules operate strictly on a **Propose-and-Confirm paradigm**. Suggestions appear as tactile candidate cards. The user or caregiver explicitly selects and confirms before any acoustic vocalization or message transmission occurs.

---

### Paper 21 & 23: Auditory Ergonomics, Speech-in-Noise, and Presbycusis
* **Key Literature:** Cambridge Intelligent Throat (*Nature Communications*, 19 Jan 2026) & Immersive Auditory-Cognitive Training (*npj Science of Learning*, 19 Nov 2025).
* **Clinical Insight:**
  Over 65% of stroke and dementia patients suffer from comorbid presbycusis (hearing loss) and sensory processing delays. Fast robotic speech output in noisy household rooms produces cognitive overload and comprehension failure.
* **What + How It Is Directly Implemented in Lisan:**
  * **File:** [`src/components/LiveCaptionsTicker.tsx`](file:///Users/alhamdulillah/codespace/lisan/src/components/LiveCaptionsTicker.tsx) & [`src/hooks/useAudio.ts`](file:///Users/alhamdulillah/codespace/lisan/src/hooks/useAudio.ts).
  * **Mechanism:** Introduces real-time, high-contrast synchronized visual captions during speech output, word-by-word highlight pacing, adjustable playback speed (0.6x, 0.8x, 1.0x), and a 1-tap "Replay / دوبارہ سنیں" instant replay button.
